## DESCRIPTION

A tiny luck app, from Adele and Kate.

White Rabbits turns an old first-of-the-month tradition into a small ritual for your phone.

On the first of every month, say "White Rabbits" before anything else and reveal that month's rabbit. There are twelve to collect across the year.

Join halfway through the month? You wait for the next one.

That is part of the magic.

Along the way, White Rabbits gives you a few quiet reasons to pause:

• collect a new rabbit on the first of each month
• leave yourself a monthly intention
• catch a little 11:11 nod from the universe
• discover daily words to carry with you
• add White Rabbits to your Home Screen with widgets
• share a little luck when the moment feels right

No streaks to protect. No feed to scroll. No account required.

Just twelve rabbits, a few tiny rituals and a little more room for luck.

Say it first.
