## What White Rabbits is (Cursor’s view)

**One sentence:** A first-of-the-month charm alarm, with a little bunny waiting on your Home Screen.

**The ritual:** In Britain (and beyond), people say “White Rabbits” as the first words of a new month for luck. They forget. The app is a proper AlarmKit alarm on the 1st that can break Silent and Focus. You say the words. You get this month’s bunny / stamp. Set it. Forget it. The phone surprises you.

**Also shipping:** Optional daily **11:11** “lucky minute” tap + quiet share card. Settings stay on-device. No account.

**Feeling:** Ladurée / Coach bag-charm precious, not a fruit keyring catalogue. Clean, calm, classy. Tallulah (Gen Z niece, TestFlight): “pilates matcha Alo vibes.” Keep that lane.

**Not the product:** Playboi Carti / dancing-cow first-of-month TikTok trends. Those are **promo later**, not Store copy and not inside the app. Tallulah said Carti song would be funny but the app is “too classy.” Trust that.

**Locked App Store line from the brief:** Charm alarm.

**Target:** App Store by **1 September 2026** (Adele’s mum’s birthday). Free at launch, then 99p once. No subscription.
