Raw candidate lines from each AI agent. Keep these even if we do not ship every line.

- `gigi-365.txt` — Gigi full list (20 Aug 2026)
- `gemini-365.txt` — Gemini full list (20 Aug 2026)

- `curation-notes.md` — what we kept / skipped and why

In-app copy is curated into `WhiteRabbits/Models/Affirmations.swift`, with the
matching editorial source in `White-Rabbits-Affirmations-Master.md`.

Only short, clear, uplifting and shareable lines belong in the app. They should
feel like a warm little nudge from the universe without sounding religious,
instructional, earned, self-improving or like a cryptic fortune cookie.
