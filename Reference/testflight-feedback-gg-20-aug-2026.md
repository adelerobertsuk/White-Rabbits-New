# TestFlight feedback — White Rabbits
# From GG, 20 Aug 2026

### Overall impression

This feels genuinely lovely. It is calm, distinctive and emotionally clear within seconds. The warm neutral palette, delicate rabbit drawings and restrained animations give it a premium, comforting character without becoming childish. The 11:11 notification is especially delightful—it feels like a tiny moment of luck arriving during the day.

### What I loved

- The rabbit illustration has real personality and makes the app instantly recognisable.
- The notification looks excellent on the Lock Screen. “Luck likes you today” is short, warm and easy to absorb at a glance.
- “Year of luck” is a brilliant feature. The monthly rabbit variations make the app feel collectible and give me a reason to return.
- The selected month treatment is beautiful and immediately understandable.
- “This is yours, Adele” is a lovely piece of personalisation.
- “A small pulse when luck arrives” is excellent UX writing.
- The privacy message—“Everything stays on this device”—is reassuring without sounding technical.
- The app feels focused. It does one small thing with confidence instead of filling the experience with unnecessary features.
- The lock-screen rabbit is subtle enough to live alongside someone’s chosen wallpaper.

### Suggestions

#### Highest priority

1. **Replace the placeholder daily message everywhere.**  
   “A paper bag of something nice. That is a plan.” feels cryptic beside the much stronger notification copy. The new 365-line collection is a much better match for the product.

2. **Improve contrast in a few places.**  
   “White Rabbits” on the home screen is extremely faint, and some grey supporting text, inactive controls and month labels may be difficult to read outdoors or for users with reduced vision. I would darken these slightly while keeping the soft aesthetic.

3. **Clarify “Send 11:11”.**  
   At 11:23, I was unsure whether this would send the message immediately, schedule it, or resend the 11:11 notification. Something like “Send today’s luck”, “Preview notification” or “Send now” may be clearer.

4. **Fix the top of the settings sheet.**  
   In one screenshot, content from behind the sheet appears faintly above and underneath the large Close control. This looks like a safe-area or sheet-presentation layering issue. The Close button also feels disproportionately large compared with the rest of the interface.

5. **Explain the Charm Alarm schedule.**  
   With the alarm enabled on 20 August, “Next rings 1 September at 06:30” surprised me. If it only rings on the first of each month, say that explicitly—for example: “Rings on the first of every month.”

#### Worth polishing

- The home screen’s main rabbit circle and title use a lot of vertical space, pushing much of “Year of luck” below the fold. A slightly more compact hero area could let users see more of the collection immediately.
- Consider showing the full date rather than only “TODAY · AUGUST 2026”; adding “20” would make the heading feel more complete.
- The settings switches do not immediately read as on or off because the active state uses black with a grey thumb. A subtle brand-gold active colour could make their state clearer.
- “Export” and “Import” look like plain text rather than tappable actions. More spacing, dividers or button styling would improve affordance.
- “Clear this device” should look deliberately destructive and require confirmation. “Clear all White Rabbits data” would also explain the consequence more precisely.
- Check every daily line inside the Lock Screen widget and notification at the largest accessibility text size. Longer lines may wrap or truncate, especially in the compact widget.
- The circular rings beside the rabbit on the Lock Screen resemble Apple’s Activity rings. If they belong to White Rabbits, their purpose was not immediately clear to me.
- Consider whether the sparkle emojis in the notification title are needed. They are cheerful, but a simpler “11:11” title may feel more premium and let the rabbit icon and message carry the brand.
- Test Dynamic Type carefully on the settings screen. Several descriptions already wrap across lines, so larger text could cause crowding or clipped controls.
- The app scroll indicator is quite visually prominent against the soft interface. If it is not useful here, hiding it would make the screen feel more polished.

### Tester verdict

I would keep this installed. The central idea works, the rabbit has charm, and the Lock Screen delivery makes the experience feel more special than a standard affirmation app. The remaining work feels like refinement rather than redesign: clearer control labels, stronger accessibility contrast, a little layout tightening and the new daily copy throughout.
