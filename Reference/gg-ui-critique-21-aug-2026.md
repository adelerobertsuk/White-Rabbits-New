# GG UI critique — White Rabbits
# 21 Aug 2026

Source: GG feedback shared in Cursor chat (post–TestFlight direction check).

### Overall

Happy with the direction. Would not make it radically cleaner or more minimal. Tighten for **hierarchy and restraint**: trim what does not earn its place, preserve whitespace, make every tap feel a little delightful. Core idea and visual identity are strong (rabbits, warm off-white, Year of luck grid, 11:11, tiny rituals). Feels like a product, not a feature demo.

Gen Z tone: keep exactly where landed. Conversational, lightly self-aware, never trying to sound “Gen Z.” Example that works: **“Okay, so this is actually a thing.”**

Litmus test for Cursor: **does every screen still feel like White Rabbits when the explanatory copy is removed?** Charm should come from object, timing, rabbit, motion, sound/haptic, and little rituals, not from text telling people how charming it is.

### App refinements (act when ready)

1. **Preferences** — doing quite a lot. Fewer words, clearer grouping.
2. **Home** — protect breathing room; strongest when it has space.
3. **Onboarding / notification permission** — explain *why* notifications matter *before* the iOS system prompt, so users do not tap Don’t Allow without knowing the little interruptions are the point.
4. **Language check with fresh eyes** — Charm Alarm, Lucky Minute, Haptics, Preview the first of the month: confirm instant understanding with people who have not seen the app.
5. **Restraint pass** — intentional, not minimalist redesign. Trim only what does not earn its place.

### Studio / website (park for another day)

- AKA Studio site: almost absurdly clean. **AKA STUDIO / We make little things.** One beautiful White Rabbits card, tiny “by AKA Studio,” footer with Support, Privacy, email.
- Feel like the app, not a generic agency site.
- **Non-negotiable:** domain, hosting, code repo, deployment account, and email all under accounts Adele and Kate control, with documented logins/recovery. No third-party dependency that can vanish if someone else revokes access.
- Personal sites rebuild later on the same principle: simple, owned, easy to maintain.

### App Store listing (park until ready)

GG offered to help with: name, subtitle, screenshots, screenshot captions, description, keywords, privacy wording, support URL, promotional text, and overall visual sequence. White Rabbits is suited to a strong listing; biggest job is explaining charm quickly without overexplaining the ritual.
