# White Rabbits

Locked 16 Aug 2026. Charm alarm. That is the App Store line.

Adele is from Southport. People say White Rabbits on the first of the month. They forget. Even her dad, a stoic Northern man, says it. Kate is from Preston. Some people say it three times. Sarah Jessica Parker says it. First words of the month, luck for the rest. Set it. Forget it. The phone surprises you.

The Ladurée / Coach / LV / Pandora bag charms are the feeling, not a shop. Kids pay mad money for a tiny precious thing on a clasp. Our bunny is that thing, on the Home Screen, for a month. Gift-box, gold, collectible. Not a fruit keyring catalogue. Keep the rabbits we already drew. They are the product.

Not a journal. Not a circle. Not a habit tracker. A delight.

## Dragon's Den

This one is real. Tiny job. Cultural hook. Family already does it. Date that matters: 1 September 2026, Adele’s mum’s birthday.

What would kill it: adding a journal, a social tab, Donnie Darko packs, a lo-fi radio, or an intention studio before the alarm actually fires.

## Ship first

1. One recurring alarm. First of every month. They pick the time. Default 6:30.
2. It must be a real alarm. iOS 26 AlarmKit. Breaks Silent and Focus. Lock Screen, like Clock.
3. The White Rabbits moment. Pretty. This month’s bunny. Say it.
4. Twelve charms. One unlocks that month. The stamp card we already designed. Collect them.
5. One Home Screen widget. This month’s bunny. Quiet. Special.

That is the whole app.

## Not now

Music library. Lo-fi. Custom songs. One bundled wake sound is enough if a song is a ballache.
Monthly intention on the widget.
Donnie Darko rabbits, boy pack, girl pack, bag-charm shop.
Phone charms as commerce.
Journal, Circle, Supabase, share cards, streaks.

iOS 26 is what ships this September, not 27. AlarmKit and a Lock Screen / Home Screen widget are the new features that matter. Foldable iPhone is rumour. Do not design for a phone that is not out. Tablo can get greedy with a big screen later.

## Keep

The bunnies we have. Palette, type, radii. Branch `swiftui-ios-app`. Same folder.

## Pause before we ship

Are we missing anything that stops the alarm from delighting someone at 6:30 on the 1st?
Can we simplify more?
If not, ship.

## How to start

Fresh chat in this folder. Paste: "Read the brief. Charm alarm. AlarmKit. Widget. Twelve stamps. App Store by 1 September if we can."
